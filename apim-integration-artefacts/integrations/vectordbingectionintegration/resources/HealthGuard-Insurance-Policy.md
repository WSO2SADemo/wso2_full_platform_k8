## 1. Plan Summary and Tiers

HealthGuard offers four health insurance plan tiers. Each member is enrolled in exactly one tier based on their application and premium selection.

| Plan Tier | Policy Number Series | Coverage Limit | Monthly Premium | Deductible | Out-of-Pocket Maximum |
|-----------|---------------------|---------------|----------------|------------|----------------------|
| Basic     | POL-HEALTH-001      | $100,000      | $120.00        | $1,500     | $5,000               |
| Standard  | POL-HEALTH-003      | $150,000      | $145.00        | $1,000     | $4,000               |
| Enhanced  | POL-HEALTH-005      | $250,000      | $180.00        | $750       | $3,000               |
| Premier   | POL-HEALTH-002, POL-HEALTH-004 | $500,000 | $350.00 | $500  | $2,000               |

**Coverage Limit** is the maximum benefit paid per policy year across all claims combined. Once the coverage limit is reached, the member bears all further costs until the policy renews.

**Deductible** is the amount the member pays out-of-pocket before HealthGuard begins to pay. The deductible resets on each policy anniversary date.

**Out-of-Pocket Maximum** is the most a member will pay in a policy year for covered services. Once reached, HealthGuard pays 100% of covered costs for the remainder of the year.

## 2. Eligibility and Enrollment

### 2.1 Eligible Members

Any individual aged 18–65 who:
- Is a legal resident in a covered territory
- Has not been diagnosed with an excluded pre-existing condition within the prior 12 months (see Section 4.3)
- Completes the enrollment application and pays the first month's premium

Dependent children up to age 26 may be added to an enrolled member's plan at an additional premium rate of 40% per dependent per month.

### 2.2 Enrollment Periods

**Open Enrollment:** November 1 – December 15 each year for coverage beginning January 1.

**Special Enrollment:** Within 30 days of a qualifying life event:
- Marriage or domestic partnership
- Birth or adoption of a child
- Loss of other health coverage
- Permanent relocation to a new coverage area

### 2.3 Waiting Period

Coverage becomes effective on the **first day of the month following enrollment** for preventive and routine services. Emergency services are covered from the **first day of the effective date**.

## 3. Covered Benefits

All benefits are subject to the deductible, coinsurance, and coverage limit of the enrolled plan tier, unless otherwise noted.

### 3.1 Preventive Care (No Cost-Sharing)

The following services are covered at **100% with no deductible** for all plan tiers:
- Annual wellness exam (one per policy year)
- Routine blood panel and lipid screening (one per policy year)
- Blood pressure, cholesterol, and diabetes screening
- Immunizations recommended by the Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP)
- Colorectal cancer screening (age 45+, one per 10 years)
- Mammography screening (women aged 40+, one per year)
- Pap smear and cervical cancer screening (one per 3 years, or annually if high-risk)
- Prostate-specific antigen (PSA) test (men aged 50+, one per year)
- Annual vision exam (one per policy year, up to $150 allowance for frames/lenses)
- Annual dental cleaning and X-rays (one per policy year, up to $200 allowance)

### 3.2 Outpatient Services

Covered at **80% after deductible** (Basic and Standard plans) or **90% after deductible** (Enhanced and Premier plans):

- Primary care physician (PCP) visits
- Specialist consultations (referral required for Basic and Standard plans)
- Urgent care center visits
- Diagnostic laboratory tests ordered by a licensed provider
- Diagnostic imaging: X-ray, ultrasound, CT scan, MRI
- Outpatient surgery and same-day procedures
- Physical therapy (up to 30 sessions per policy year)
- Occupational therapy (up to 20 sessions per policy year)
- Speech therapy (up to 20 sessions per policy year)
- Chiropractic care (up to 15 sessions per policy year)
- Mental health and behavioral health outpatient visits (up to 52 sessions per policy year)
- Substance use disorder outpatient treatment
- Telehealth and virtual care visits

**Claim Type Code for Outpatient Billing:** `Outpatient`

### 3.3 Inpatient Hospitalization

Covered at **70% after deductible** (Basic plan), **80% after deductible** (Standard and Enhanced plans), or **90% after deductible** (Premier plan):

- Inpatient admission and room charges (semi-private room standard; private room covered if medically necessary)
- Intensive Care Unit (ICU) or Cardiac Care Unit (CCU) stays
- Inpatient surgery, anesthesia, and recovery room
- Inpatient nursing care
- Hospital-administered medications and IV therapy
- Inpatient diagnostic testing
- Inpatient physical rehabilitation following surgery or acute illness
- Newborn care for the first 72 hours following delivery (mother's admission)

Pre-authorization is **required** for all non-emergency inpatient admissions. Failure to obtain pre-authorization may result in a 20% reduction in the benefit paid.

**Claim Type Code for Inpatient Billing:** `Hospitalization`

### 3.4 Emergency Services

Covered at **80% after deductible** for all plan tiers regardless of network status:

- Emergency room visits for life-threatening or serious conditions
- Emergency ambulance transport (ground or air when medically necessary)
- Emergency surgery and stabilization
- Emergency mental health crisis intervention

**Important:** A non-emergency visit to an emergency room is subject to a $250 facility fee that does not apply toward the deductible. HealthGuard reserves the right to review emergency room claims for medical necessity.

### 3.5 Maternity and Newborn Care

Covered for all enrolled members and eligible dependents:

- Prenatal visits (unlimited, no specialist referral required)
- Prenatal lab work and ultrasounds
- Inpatient labor and delivery
- Postpartum care (up to 6 visits within 60 days of delivery)
- Newborn hospital stay (covered under the mother's hospitalization benefit for the first 72 hours; thereafter, newborn must be enrolled as a dependent)
- Lactation consultation (up to 6 sessions)

### 3.6 Prescription Drug Coverage

Prescription benefits are managed through the HealthGuard Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM). Members must use a participating pharmacy.

### 3.7 Mental Health and Substance Use Disorder

HealthGuard provides parity between mental health and medical/surgical benefits:

- Inpatient psychiatric hospitalization (covered at the same rate as medical inpatient)
- Residential treatment programs (up to 30 days per policy year, requires pre-authorization)
- Intensive outpatient programs (IOP): up to 60 sessions per policy year
- Outpatient therapy: up to 52 sessions per policy year (see Section 3.2)
- Medication-assisted treatment (MAT) for substance use disorders
- Psychological testing (up to 8 hours per policy year)
- Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) therapy for autism spectrum disorder (up to 40 hours per week for members under 21)

### 3.8 Chronic Disease Management

Members with diagnosed chronic conditions are eligible for additional support services at no cost-sharing:

- Diabetes management program (glucometer, test strips, and insulin covered at Tier 1 copay)
- Hypertension monitoring program
- Asthma and COPD management (nebulizer equipment covered under durable medical equipment)
- Cardiac rehabilitation (up to 36 sessions following qualifying cardiac event)
- Oncology case management for active cancer treatment

### 3.9 Durable Medical Equipment (DME)

Covered at **80% after deductible** (all tiers) with prior authorization for items over $500:

- Wheelchairs (manual and powered, where medically necessary)
- Hospital-grade beds (rental only, up to 12 months per episode)
- CPAP and BiPAP devices for sleep apnea
- Prosthetics and orthotics
- Blood glucose monitoring equipment
- Oxygen equipment and supplies
- Nebulizers and respiratory equipment

## 4. Exclusions and Limitations

HealthGuard does **not** cover the following services. Members are solely responsible for costs related to excluded services.

### 4.1 General Exclusions

- Services not medically necessary as defined by HealthGuard's Medical Policy
- Experimental or investigational procedures not approved by an accredited clinical body
- Cosmetic surgery or procedures performed solely for aesthetic purposes (rhinoplasty, blepharoplasty, liposuction, etc.)
- Services received from a non-participating provider without prior authorization (except emergency services)
- Services outside the policy's geographic coverage area, unless emergency
- Over-the-counter (OTC) medications, vitamins, supplements, and herbal remedies
- Weight loss programs, medications, and bariatric surgery (exception: covered under Enhanced and Premier plans with documented BMI ≥40 and prior authorization)
- Infertility treatments, including IVF, IUI, and fertility medications
- Genetic testing not directed by a licensed physician for diagnostic purposes
- Long-term custodial or nursing home care

### 4.2 Dental and Vision Exclusions

The annual preventive dental and vision allowances (Section 3.1) do not cover:
- Orthodontic treatment (braces, aligners)
- Dental implants, crowns, bridges, or dentures
- Periodontal surgery
- Prescription eyeglasses or contact lenses beyond the $150 annual allowance
- Laser vision correction (LASIK, PRK)

### 4.3 Pre-Existing Condition Limitations

In accordance with applicable law, HealthGuard does **not** impose pre-existing condition exclusions for members enrolled through the standard Open or Special Enrollment periods. However, the following conditions are subject to a **12-month waiting period** if first diagnosed or treated within 6 months before the coverage effective date:

- Organ transplant (waiting period applies to non-emergency transplants)
- Joint replacement surgery (hip, knee, shoulder) — Basic plan only
- Spinal fusion surgery — Basic plan only

## 5. Network Providers

### 5.1 In-Network Providers

HealthGuard maintains a network of hospitals, clinics, physicians, and specialists under the **HealthGuard Preferred Provider Organization (PPO)**. Members receive the highest level of benefits when using in-network providers.

To find an in-network provider: providers.healthguard-insurance.com or call 1-800-555-4747.

### 5.2 Out-of-Network Benefits

Out-of-network services are covered at **50% of the Allowed Amount** after a separate out-of-network deductible of $3,000 (individual) / $6,000 (family). The out-of-network out-of-pocket maximum is $10,000 per policy year.

**Exception:** Emergency services are always covered at in-network benefit levels regardless of provider network status.

### 5.3 Referrals

- **Basic and Standard plans** require a Primary Care Physician (PCP) referral for specialist visits.
- **Enhanced and Premier plans** do not require referrals for in-network specialist visits.

### 5.4 Primary Care Physician (PCP) Selection

Members enrolled in Basic and Standard plans must designate a PCP at the time of enrollment. PCP changes are permitted once per quarter. The PCP is responsible for coordinating care and issuing referrals to specialists.

## 6. How to File a Claim

### 6.1 In-Network Claims

When a member receives care from an in-network provider, the provider typically files the claim directly with HealthGuard. The member is responsible only for their applicable copay, coinsurance, and deductible at the time of service.

### 6.2 Out-of-Network or Member-Submitted Claims

If a member receives care from an out-of-network provider or pays out-of-pocket, they may file a claim for reimbursement:

**Step 1 — Obtain Itemized Receipt**
Request an itemized statement from the provider showing: provider name and NPI number, date of service, diagnosis code (ICD-10), procedure code (CPT), and the amount charged.

**Step 2 — Complete the Claim Form**
Download the HealthGuard Member Claim Form from claims.healthguard-insurance.com or request one by calling 1-800-555-4747.

**Step 3 — Submit the Claim**
Submit the completed form and itemized receipt by:
- **Online:** claims.healthguard-insurance.com (preferred, fastest processing)
- **Email:** claims@healthguard-insurance.com (PDF attachments only, max 20 MB)
- **Mail:** HealthGuard Claims Department, PO Box 88200, Hartford, CT 06101
- **Fax:** 1-860-555-0199

**Step 4 — Receive Explanation of Benefits (EOB)**
Within 15 business days of claim receipt, HealthGuard will issue an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) detailing: the billed amount, the allowed amount, deductible applied, coinsurance owed, and the amount paid or denied.

### 6.3 Claim Filing Deadline

Claims must be submitted within **365 days** of the date of service. Claims submitted after this deadline will be denied.

### 6.4 Claim Type Codes

When submitting a claim, use the appropriate claim type:

| Situation | Claim Type Code |
|-----------|----------------|
| Doctor visit, specialist, lab, imaging | `Outpatient` |
| Inpatient hospital stay, surgery | `Hospitalization` |
| Emergency room visit | `Emergency` |
| Prescription drug (out-of-network pharmacy) | `Pharmacy` |
| Medical equipment purchase | `DME` |
| Mental health outpatient | `Outpatient` |
| Mental health inpatient | `Hospitalization` |
| Other medical services | `Medical` |

## 7. Claim Status and Appeals

### 7.1 Checking Claim Status

Members can check claim status at any time:
- **Online:** claims.healthguard-insurance.com → "My Claims"
- **Phone:** 1-800-555-4747 (Mon–Fri 8 AM–8 PM EST)
- **Mobile App:** HealthGuard Member App (iOS and Android)

Claims are assigned one of the following statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `PENDING` | Claim received and under review |
| `APPROVED` | Claim approved; payment issued or applied to deductible |
| `REJECTED` | Claim denied; see EOB for reason code |

### 7.2 Denial Reasons (Common)

| Reason Code | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| NOT_COVERED | Service is excluded under the member's plan |
| NO_AUTH | Pre-authorization was required but not obtained |
| OUT_OF_NETWORK | Provider is out-of-network and out-of-network benefits do not apply |
| FILING_DEADLINE | Claim submitted after the 365-day filing deadline |
| DUPLICATE | Duplicate claim already processed |
| MEDICAL_NECESSITY | Service not deemed medically necessary |
| COB | Coordination of Benefits — other payer is primary |

### 7.3 Appeals Process

Members have the right to appeal any adverse benefit determination within **180 days** of receiving the EOB.

**Level 1 — Internal Appeal**
Submit a written appeal with supporting documentation (physician letter, medical records, literature) to:
HealthGuard Appeals Department, PO Box 88201, Hartford, CT 06101
Or email: appeals@healthguard-insurance.com

HealthGuard will respond within **30 calendar days** (non-urgent) or **72 hours** (urgent/expedited).

**Level 2 — External Review**
If the Level 1 internal appeal is upheld, the member may request an External Review by an Independent Review Organization (IRO) accredited by URAC. The IRO's decision is binding on HealthGuard.

Premiums are due on the **1st of each month** for that month's coverage. A grace period of **30 days** is provided before coverage is suspended.

### 8.3 Payment Methods

- Automatic bank draft (ACH) — recommended, 5% discount applied
- Credit or debit card via member portal
- Check payable to "HealthGuard Insurance Corporation"

### 8.4 Consequences of Non-Payment

Coverage is **suspended** after 30 days of non-payment (status changes to `SUSPENDED`). Claims incurred during a suspension period are not covered. Coverage is **terminated** after 60 days of non-payment (status changes to `EXPIRED`). Reinstatement requires payment of all outstanding premiums plus a $75 reinstatement fee, subject to underwriting review.

## 9. Policy Renewal and Termination

### 9.1 Automatic Renewal

Policies automatically renew on the anniversary date (e.g., a policy starting January 1, 2024 renews on January 1, 2025) unless the member notifies HealthGuard of cancellation at least 30 days before renewal.

Renewal premiums may change. HealthGuard will notify members of any rate change at least **60 days** before the renewal date.

### 9.2 Voluntary Cancellation

Members may cancel coverage by submitting a written cancellation request to customer.service@healthguard-insurance.com or by calling 1-800-555-4747. Coverage ends on the last day of the month in which the request is received.

### 9.3 Involuntary Termination

HealthGuard may terminate coverage for:
- Non-payment of premium (see Section 8.4)
- Fraud or misrepresentation on the application
- The member no longer resides in the coverage area
- Discontinuation of the product in the market (12 months' notice required)

### 9.4 Continuation of Coverage (COBRA)

If employment-sponsored coverage is terminated, members may be eligible for continuation under applicable state or federal continuation laws. Contact HealthGuard for continuation election forms within 30 days of the qualifying event.

## 10. Definitions

**Allowed Amount:** The maximum amount HealthGuard will pay for a covered service based on the contracted rate (in-network) or the usual and customary charge (out-of-network).

**Coinsurance:** The percentage of covered costs the member pays after the deductible is met (e.g., 20% coinsurance means HealthGuard pays 80%).

**Copay:** A fixed dollar amount the member pays for a specific covered service, typically at the time of service.

**Coverage Limit:** The maximum total benefit HealthGuard will pay for all covered services within a policy year. Amounts paid toward the deductible and coinsurance by the member do not count toward the coverage limit.

**Deductible:** The amount a member must pay for covered services before HealthGuard begins to share costs. Preventive care is not subject to the deductible.

**Explanation of Benefits (EOB):** A statement issued by HealthGuard after a claim is processed, showing how the claim was adjudicated.

**In-Network:** A provider that has a contract with HealthGuard to provide services at negotiated rates.

**Medically Necessary:** Services or supplies needed to diagnose or treat an illness, injury, condition, disease, or its symptoms and that meet accepted standards of medicine.

**Out-of-Network:** A provider that does not have a contract with HealthGuard. Higher cost-sharing applies for out-of-network services.

**Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** The most a member will pay for covered in-network services during a policy year. After this amount is reached, HealthGuard pays 100% of covered costs.

**Pre-Authorization (Prior Authorization):** Approval required from HealthGuard before receiving certain services, procedures, or medications. Obtaining pre-authorization does not guarantee payment; claims are still subject to all policy terms at the time of adjudication.

**Premium:** The monthly amount a member pays to maintain health insurance coverage, regardless of whether they use medical services.
