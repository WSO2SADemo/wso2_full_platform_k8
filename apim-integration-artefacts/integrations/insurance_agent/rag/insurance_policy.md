# SureSafe Insurance — Standard Health Policy (Policy No: SS-HEALTH-001)

## 1. Coverage Overview
This policy provides comprehensive health coverage for the named insured and
eligible dependents. Coverage includes hospitalization, outpatient care,
prescription drugs, and emergency services worldwide.

## 2. Deductibles and Co-payments
- Annual deductible: $500 per individual / $1,000 per family
- Co-payment: 20% after deductible for in-network; 40% out-of-network
- Out-of-pocket maximum: $3,000 individual / $6,000 family

## 3. Covered Services
- Inpatient hospitalization: up to 60 days per year
- Emergency room visits: covered after $150 co-pay
- Preventive care (annual checkup, vaccines): 100% covered, no deductible
- Mental health and substance abuse: 30 sessions per year
- Maternity: prenatal, delivery, and 6-week postpartum covered
- Prescription drugs: Tier 1 $10 / Tier 2 $30 / Tier 3 $60 co-pay

## 4. Exclusions
- Cosmetic surgery not medically required
- Experimental or investigational treatments
- Pre-existing conditions within the first 12 months (waiting period)
- Dental and vision (covered under separate riders)
- Long-term custodial care

## 5. Claims Process
1. Obtain pre-authorization for elective procedures over $500
2. Submit claim form within 90 days of service
3. Attach itemized bill and Explanation of Benefits (EOB)
4. Claims are processed within 30 business days
5. Appeals must be filed within 60 days of denial

## 6. Premiums and Renewals
- Monthly premium: $320 individual / $780 family
- Policy renews annually on January 1
- 30-day grace period for late payments
- Premium increases capped at 5% per year for continuous policyholders

## 7. Emergency Coverage Abroad
- Emergency stabilization covered worldwide
- Reimbursement at in-network rates
- Must notify insurer within 48 hours of hospitalization

## 8. Cancellation Policy
- Policyholder may cancel with 30 days written notice
- Pro-rated refund for unused premium
- Insurer may cancel for non-payment or fraud
